# Ritual — log (the running record)

Between boot and close, keep a running record of what actually happened —
decisions, commits, dead ends, findings. The baton at close is **written from
this record, not from memory** (SPEC §2.1: the baton quotes disk).

Where the record lives is the host's choice: a house journal, the tracker, a
scratch file. The only rule is that it is *on disk* by the time you close, so the
baton indexes something real.
